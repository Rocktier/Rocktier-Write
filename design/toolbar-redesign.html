Chapter One — Ebb

The library opens only at low tide. The keeper says the shelves extend as far as the water withdraws, and the rows that drown at high tide reopen with the next full moon.

On the third row after the ebb, she found the book. Its spine carried no title, yet it felt warm, the way paper feels when someone has only just closed it.

  • Words, not guesses. Nine of ten controls now carry a word; the only bare icon is the sidebar toggle, pinned to the brand like a window control.
  • Three verb groups. Create-and-bring-in (New, Open, Import) sits left; leave-the-document (Save, PDF) right; tools (Find, Goal, Focus, Theme) far right. Hairlines mark the seams.
  • The middle is gone. Focus moved into Tools; the status bar still shows the fuller "Focus: Paragraph" state, so nothing is lost.
  • Recent lives in Open ▾. Click opens a file; the chevron lists the recent ones. One clock fewer, one button fewer.
  • Unsaved state is a small dot inside the Save button, next to the word, instead of a red speck floating over the floppy.
  • Narrow windows (<1240px): Tools-group labels fold first, then Create/Open labels — icons and tooltips remain, same as today.
