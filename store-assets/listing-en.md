# Rocktier Write — Microsoft Store listing (English, master)

**Short description (≤ 253 chars)**
A distraction-free writing app for long-form work. Everything stays on your machine — no account, no cloud, no sync. Your manuscript never leaves your device.

**Description**

Write the long thing. Novels, memoirs, theses — Rocktier Write is built for work measured in chapters, not notes.

**Private by architecture.** Rocktier Write is 100% offline. There is no account to create, no cloud to sync to, no telemetry phoning home. Your manuscript is saved as plain Markdown files in folders you control, and it never leaves your machine unless you put it there. For a writer, that is not a feature — it is the precondition for writing freely.

**Built for chapters.** A chapter sidebar tracks every heading with per-chapter word counts and goals. Focus mode dims everything except the paragraph or sentence you are writing. A typewriter scroll keeps your eyes where the words are.

**From draft to submission.** Write in clean Markdown with a live preview, then hand in what the publisher asked for — Word (.docx), PDF, or plain text — all rendered on your own machine.

**Details**
- Formats: Markdown (.md) in and out; Word (.docx) and PDF export
- Word goals with per-chapter progress
- 8 interface languages
- Works fully offline, forever
